Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about Student Hub

How do I register for an event?
Navigating to the Events page from the main menu allows you to browse all upcoming campus events. Click on the "Register Now" button under any event card to reserve your spot instantly.
Is it free to join campus events?
Yes! The vast majority of student events, workshops, hackathons, and guest lectures hosted on Student Hub are 100% free for registered university students.
How can I update my profile information?
Log in to your account and click on Profile in the student sidebar. You can update your course details, phone number, residential address, and profile photo anytime.
Where can I find study resources?
Study resources, mid-term exam schedules, lab manuals, and previous year question papers can be accessed directly from your Student Dashboard under the Resources tab.
How can I contact support?
You can reach out to our campus support team through our Contact Us page, or email us directly at support@studenthub.edu.
How do I reset my password?
On the Login page, click the "Forgot Password?" link below the password input field. Follow the on-screen instructions to receive a password reset link in your student email.
